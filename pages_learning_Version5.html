Learning — 10 resources & strategies

How to learn effectively and which resources to rely on.

  1. Project-based learning — Build small projects to apply concepts practically.
  2. Deliberate practice — Focus on weaknesses with targeted exercises.
  3. Online courses (Coursera, Udemy) — Structured paths for new topics.
  4. Reading lists — Curate books and articles for deep understanding.
  5. Community — Join study groups or forums to get feedback and accountability.
  6. Mentorship — Pair with experienced people for faster learning and shortcuts.
  7. Cheat sheets & summaries — Keep concise references for recurring tasks.
  8. Teach others — Explaining concepts reinforces your own understanding.
  9. Scheduled review — Use spaced repetition for long-term retention.
  10. Playlists & curated tutorials — Follow a progressive path from basics to advanced topics.
