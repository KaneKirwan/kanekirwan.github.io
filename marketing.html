Marketing — 10 tactics & tools

Practical marketing ideas, channels, and supporting tools.

  1. Content calendar — Plan topics and repurpose across channels for consistent reach.
  2. SEO tools (Ahrefs, Google Search Console) — Track keywords and performance to drive organic traffic.
  3. Email automation (Mailchimp, ConvertKit) — Nurture leads with segmented sequences.
  4. Social scheduling — Batch content and schedule to maintain presence efficiently.
  5. Analytics (Google Analytics, GA4) — Measure funnels and conversion rates carefully.
  6. A/B testing — Iterate landing pages and CTAs to improve conversions.
  7. Customer interviews — Qualitative research to inform messaging and positioning.
  8. Referral programs — Incentivize advocates to grow organically.
  9. Marketing automation (HubSpot) — Centralize leads, scoring, and lifecycle orchestration.
  10. Creative assets library — Maintain on-brand templates and approved media for fast production.
