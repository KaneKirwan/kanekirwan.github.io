Productivity — 10 workflows & tools

Work smarter with repeatable systems and the right tools.

  1. Notion / Roam — Centralize notes, docs, and project plans with templates.
  2. Todoist / Things — Task management with prioritization and recurring tasks.
  3. Calendar blocking — Schedule deep work and protect focus time.
  4. Keyboard shortcuts & snippets — Save time on repetitive text and actions.
  5. Automations (IFTTT, Zapier) — Connect apps to remove manual steps.
  6. Time tracking — Measure where time is spent to optimize processes.
  7. Meeting templates — Use agendas and clear outcomes to keep meetings efficient.
  8. Regular retrospectives — Iterate on team processes and remove friction.
  9. Focus tools (Pomodoro apps) — Break work into focused sprints and rest intervals.
  10. Documentation-as-code — Keep docs versioned with code and in CI checks.
