Writing — 10 techniques & tools

Clear, effective writing practices and the tools to support them.

  1. Outlines — Structure ideas first to keep writing focused and scannable.
  2. Headlines and ledes — Hook readers early with clear value and benefit statements.
  3. Style guide — Maintain consistent voice and formatting across content.
  4. Grammar tools (Grammarly) — Catch clarity and grammar issues quickly.
  5. Readable formatting — Use subheads, bullets, and short paragraphs for scanability.
  6. Templates — Reuse templates for blog posts, case studies, and release notes.
  7. Content editing workflow — Peer reviews and version control for major pieces.
  8. CMS (Static site) — Publish content with a simple workflow, prebuilt templates, and CI deploys.
  9. SEO copy basics — Target a single keyword cluster and write for humans first.
  10. Republishing strategy — Update and repromote high-value content instead of constantly creating new pieces.
